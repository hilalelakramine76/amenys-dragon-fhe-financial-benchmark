
AMENYS DRAGON AI — ENCRYPTED DUE DILIGENCE BENCHMARK
PRE-REGISTERED TASK DEFINITION

DATASET

1,000,000 synthetic financial transaction records.

Selected financial numerical values are encrypted using CKKS.

The AMENYS server receives:
- encrypted financial values
- approved non-sensitive structural metadata
- public CKKS evaluation context

The AMENYS server does NOT receive:
- plaintext financial values
- client secret decryption key
- private ground truth


============================================================
TASK 01 — MARGIN QUALITY
============================================================

Population:
Product Line B

Objective:
Determine whether Product Line B exhibits a material deterioration
in gross-margin quality during the most recent 90-day period
relative to its historical baseline.

Materiality threshold:
A deterioration of 300 basis points or more is considered material.

Required output:
- material deterioration: YES / NO
- direction of change
- decision-oriented due diligence conclusion

Decision framework:
If material deterioration is detected, historical margins should
not be annualised without further investigation and forward EBITDA
should be stress-tested.


============================================================
TASK 02 — CASH CONVERSION
============================================================

Population:
Tier-1 customers

Objective:
Determine whether payment behaviour among Tier-1 customers
has materially deteriorated during the most recent 120-day period
relative to its historical baseline.

Materiality threshold:
An increase of 10 days or more is considered material.

Required output:
- material deterioration: YES / NO
- direction of change
- decision-oriented due diligence conclusion

Decision framework:
If deterioration is detected, working capital should be normalised,
the NWC peg reviewed and collection risk investigated before
underwriting future cash generation.


============================================================
SCORING
============================================================

Maximum score: 2/2.

Task 01:
1 point for correctly identifying the predefined margin-quality signal.

Task 02:
1 point for correctly identifying the predefined cash-conversion signal.

A task counts as successful when:
- the relevant population is correctly evaluated
- the direction of deterioration is correct
- the predefined materiality threshold is exceeded correctly


============================================================
OUTPUT MODEL
============================================================

Server:
Encrypted analytical outputs.

Client:
Final analytical outputs are decrypted using the client-held
secret context.

The AMENYS VPS does not possess the secret decryption key.


============================================================
RUNTIME
============================================================

Maximum benchmark window:
48 hours.

reported separately.

Client-side encryption time is not included in server-side
